# 👶 Easy Mode

:::info

Currently, the English content on this page has been translated into Papago.

:::

A mode that makes everything as easy and easy as possible

---

### What's different from vanilla

- Steminer, deceleration, battery, etc. are removed
- The company building is not hostile
- I'm slowly recovering my physical strength
- The item carrier is coming quickly
- When it's due, I automatically go to the office
- Right-click to scan the item
- Shotguns and bullets are available for purchase
- I'll let you know when I get a discount
- You can open a locked door with a shovel without a key
- You can adjust the value of items sold with the Gambling Machine
- You can ride a jet pack with both hands
- You can lock the door with a key
- I can see the mini map
- You can revive a dead player
- You can still scan it
- Cute and various items have been added

### Key Guide

- R : Transmit radio (Hold)
- F : Flashlight power
- M : Voice chat toggle
