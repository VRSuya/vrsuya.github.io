### The difference between vanilla and game logic

- Upgrade capabilities and inventory slots with private XP and ship XP
- In more difficult weather, the amount and average value of scrap metal inside the facility goes up
- You can extend the deadline by paying an additional fee
- You can order more than a dozen items from the portable terminal
- You can check the xanthan of the shotgun with the reload button
- You can kill a dog without eyes with a shotgun
- You can catch a mob and sell it to the company building
- The mask has the same appearance as the player
- The mask may emote
- Turrets and landmines respond to mobs
- In the company building, you can store both hands in inventory without restriction
- There is no limit to the number of items that can be sold at a time in the company building
- If you put things on the ship, merge the same type (optimized)

### Key Guide

- R : Transmit radio (hold)
- F : Flashlight power
- V : Transmit headset radio (hold)
- M : Voice chat toggle
- X : Portable terminal (improved capability, etc.)
- Alt + Wheel : Switch expansion slot

### $10,000 Game Changes for Lethal Company

- Monster maximum spawn power is 150%
- Scrap spawn is 300%
- Scrap value is 150%
- Price of all purchased items increased by 10x
- All the planets are 25 percent bigger overall
- Every paid planet has a five-fold increase in costs
- Experience value is 10%
- Company building, **Experimentation only will be free planet**
- Experimentation, Assurance, Vow planets $1100, scrap quality additional correction (300 * 300%)
- Offence, March Planet $1800; scrap value corrected by 200% more (150 * 200%)
- Rend, Dine and Titan planets have Monster Max Spawn Power corrected by 150% extra (150 * 150%), Spawn Probability by 200%
- All plants **spawn average of $5000 in clear weather**
- Other than that, it's the same as vanilla
