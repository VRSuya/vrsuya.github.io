### 📦 Digital products
This store sells digital products. **Digital products cannot be refunded or exchanged.** Before purchasing, be sure to carefully choose whether to purchase or not and to make sure that you purchase the correct option.

### 🧒 Avatar-only products
Also, in the case of avatar-only products, it is only available for the avatar, so it is not possible to use it in other avatars and combined cases, so it is impossible to respond to the refund request.
